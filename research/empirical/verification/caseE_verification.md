Second analyst. Verified 2026-11-22.

**Method.** I downloaded the sources again with curl into a new, empty folder (`scratchpad/caseE_src_v2/`) and extracted the text locally:
- CAIB: `pdftotext -layout`.
- Rogers: an HTML-to-text pass.

I did not use a summarizing fetcher, so every "actual wording" below is copied from my own extraction. Page numbers were located with a phrase-search script (`scratchpad/caseE_scripts/find.py`).

**Page conventions.**
- **CAIB.** I used the PDF page index. On every page I checked, the index matched the printed footer (e.g., PDF p. 126 ends "126 Report Volume I August 2003"; the same holds for pp. 173 and 174).
- **Rogers.** The HTML transcription embeds markers of the form `[ 120 ]`. I confirmed that a marker comes **before** the content of the page it names:
  - Ch. VI opens "[ 120 ] The Space Shuttle's Solid Rocket Booster problem began…".
  - Ch. V opens "[ 82 ] The decision to launch the Challenger was flawed…".
  - A passage is therefore assigned to the last marker that precedes it.

## Discrepancies found in the extraction

1. **Aug 19, 1985 HQ briefing ("safe to continue flying").** The extractor cites p.140. It is **Rogers Figure 5, p.139**. The page is wrong; the wording is right.
2. **The 1983 waiver was granted by NASA Levels I and II** (p.127), not Level I only. This bears on authority: the formal relaxation was taken at the top of the program, with a stated rationale (the 1980 certification review) that is not flight success.
3. **Mulloy "24 previous times" (p.96).** The wording is a retrospective reconstruction ("may have been expressed by me using some of those words"). The tag should be RETROSPECTIVE testimony, not contemporaneous reasoning.
4. **The rescue study (CAIB p.173) assumed inconclusive imagery and an EVA inspection on FD5.** The extractor's Q4-11 omits this. It is load-bearing for the Section 3 test: the documented viable path ran through a crew inspection within NASA's own authority, not through the clearance-restricted national imaging.
5. **Erosion "safety margin" sentence.** It is on Rogers p.133, and the 41-C Level I approval is on p.132. The extractor's Q2-3 already cites both correctly. The brief's "p.132" refers to the approval.
6. **CAIB p.196 vs Rogers on criticality direction.** Confirmed as a conflict (V19). Rogers' dated record controls.

No fabricated or misattributed quotation was found. Every quotation checked matches my own extraction, apart from the hyphenation artifacts that the extractor disclosed.
