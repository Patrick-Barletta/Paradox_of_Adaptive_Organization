## Outcome

Of 22 checks (V22 bundles further report citations), 20 are fully verified, 1 is partly verified (V18: the BoE printed page numbers are off by one in the extractor's file, though the PDF page numbers are correct), and 1 sub-claim is unverified (V1: the "Key Mechanics" attribution, which is not load-bearing). I found no fabricated or altered quotes. Every load-bearing data-availability claim held up on independent re-fetch:

- the SR477 and SR506 windows (Jul 2008 to Jan 2010);
- the fn.14 gap;
- the absence of margin series in the primary dealer statistics;
- the IMF and CGFS tables as two-point snapshots.

Caveats that carry into the report:
- I could not reach the live IMF URL; my IMF copy is the Wayback capture.
- In SR506 the "Stable" period is post-crisis (Jul 2009 to Jan 2010) and is not a pre-crisis baseline. The extractor recorded this correctly; any reader comparing 2.8 vs 2.7 should keep it in mind.
