## New negative observation (not in extractor's file)

- **The RCA has no recovery-side finding or mitigation.** I searched S3 for remediat/reboot/restart/recover/rollback/revert/safe mode. There is no discussion of recovering crashed hosts and no mitigation that adds a recovery path for hosts disabled by content. Every Finding addresses prevention (validation, testing, bounds checks) or blast radius (staged rings, customer delivery control). S3's only rollback language is fleet-level: Template Instances "rolled back if problems are detected" (Finding 6, p.6). This is an absence finding in one vendor document. It does not show that no recovery work happened elsewhere.

21 of 21 checked facts verified as quoted. One ledger URL defect: the S6d `/en-us/` URL returns 404, and the non-`/en-us/` URL works. One date caveat: S1's 24 July publication date is not shown on the page itself. I found no fabricated or materially misquoted evidence.
