# Falsification results

Each positive or partial result in the five case reports was put to the adversarial test the execution spec names, and against the negative controls the sources document. Null and negative results are listed with the same prominence as positive ones. No parameter was introduced to make a historical outcome fit.

## 1. The required tests

| Case | Test | Result |
|---|---|---|
| A Boeing | Can the failure be explained without accumulated serial recovery latency? | **Yes.** No dated modification is documented as adding a mandatory recovery step (M2 = 0 against the pre-registered minimum of 2). The in-flight recovery structure is a set of alternatives (KNKT pp.193–194). Every documented step ordering was created by the 2019–2020 remediation itself, and its corrective actions are unordered and partly parallel. The investigations attribute the accidents to specific design assumptions, the single AOA input, maintenance and crew factors (KNKT p.215), and to oversight, communication and cost/schedule pressure (JATR; House report). **H1B (Escape Ratchet) fails its minimum evidence.** |
| B 2003 blackout | Can the event be explained by situational-awareness and protection failures without a repeated ratchet? | **Yes, and this is the best-supported account.** FirstEnergy held the authority and written procedures to shed load (TF pp.62, 67); both investigations attribute inaction to not knowing (TF pp.62, 67; NERC pp.94–97). No dated prior intervention adds a recovery step (M2 = 0), so the repeated-patching mechanism is not testable. The shared-dependency hypothesis (H2) cannot be distinguished from the rival because FirstEnergy's time to shed load is undocumented. |
| C CrowdStrike | Would independent recovery materially change the outcome once endpoints were disabled? | **Not as documented.** Independent recovery paths existed (Safe Mode/WinRE on day 0; external boot media from 20–22 July; snapshot rollback for VMs). Every physical-endpoint form required hands at the machine and often a BitLocker key. The levers that change the outcome (earlier halt, staged rollout, customer control of delivery) act on *which* endpoints crash, not on recovery. Only a remotely triggerable, key-free recovery environment would plausibly change recovery, and none is documented as available, so it remains a labelled hypothetical. |
| D Financial crisis | Do protective margins actually erode during calm, or is the mechanism exposure growth or funding withdrawal? | **Cannot be tested; the quantitative claim is stopped.** No primary source has a dated pre-crisis margin series. In the best-measured segment (tri-party, from July 2008), margins were "surprisingly stable for most borrowers" and the failure mechanism was lenders withdrawing funding, not margin insufficiency (SR506). Exposure growth is documented with a contemporaneous primary series (dealer repo financing roughly $0.9tn in 2001 to $2.3tn in mid-2007), but without margins it cannot be separated from H4. |
| E NASA | Do the records show altered safety policy, or persistent hazards and failures of engineering judgment? | **Altered practice, not altered policy.** No formal acceptance criterion was relaxed on the ground of prior success in either program: Challenger's formal criticality became stricter in 1982, and Columbia's debris requirement never changed. Executed thresholds did drift, with prior success explicitly cited at dated decisions (Rogers pp.137–138, 147; CAIB pp.125–126). The engineering-judgment rival overlaps with H5 rather than competing with it, because the documented judgments are themselves experience-based. The schedule, communication and customer-pressure rivals are documented as co-equal or decisive, so causal identification is not reached. |

## 2. Negative controls

| Case | Documented control | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| A | The 2019–2020 remediation added complexity (FCC cross-monitoring, an AOA split monitor, eight AFM procedures) while the FAA found no failure mode relying on "immediate pilot actions" (FAA RTS summary p.13). AD actions (j) and (k) add work but no serial step. Foreign validation ran concurrently. | Added complexity without a longer recovery critical path. Consistent with the framework's statement that only serial prerequisites count; does not support a ratchet in this case. |
| A | 201 significant design changes certified change by change (House report p.43; JATR p.8), none documented as adding a recovery prerequisite. | Weak control: complexity without a documented recovery-path effect. |
| B | PJM, New York and New England held higher voltages and more reactive reserves and separated automatically, "No human intervention" (TF pp.90–92); the western New York island survived (TF p.100). | Areas that held were distinguished by voltage/reactive margin and topology, not by governance structure. Points away from H2's governance component. |
| C | Hosts offline during 04:09–05:27 UTC, hosts connecting after the revert, Mac and Linux hosts: not affected. | Supports the exposure-window account of which hosts crashed. No control exists for recovery architecture (no recovery times split by VM vs physical). |
| D | Tri-party margins stable through the crisis window; Treasury margins unchanged; Bank of England (April 2007) reported hedge-fund leverage had fallen since 2000; portfolio margin models calibrated to stressed periods (CGFS 36). | Counter-evidence to a broad haircut-erosion narrative in the measured segments. |
| E | STS-27R (1988): crew directed to inspect, anomalies assigned, two years after Challenger; STS-107 (2003): inspection declined after 87 missions without a major incident (CAIB pp.127, 199). | The closest analogue to "same shock after short vs long calm", and confounded (different program state, robotic arm availability, leadership). Cannot isolate calm length. |

## 3. Findings that contradict or narrow the theory's proposed applications

1. **The Escape Ratchet (Theorem VI.1) was not found in either case where it was tested.** Boeing and the 2003 blackout each have M2 = 0. Complexity, design deficiency and long remediation are documented; accumulating serial recovery prerequisites are not.
2. **No severity-3 classification is established in any case.** Wherever the organizational-restriction condition could be evaluated (Boeing post-accident, the blackout, Columbia, Challenger), the protective alternative was within the authority of those who declined it. The documented failures sit in policy-class choices, information (the blackout's alarm and state-estimator failures), engineering judgment, or physics, not in organizational exclusion of a viable physical alternative.
3. **Information failure is not captured by the four control classes.** The blackout's documented failure lies in observation: the policy that would have acted (shed load) was admissible and within authority, but the operators did not know it was needed. The framework handles this only through I.3.8's observation-based strategies. It is an interpretation, not a classification the case can establish, and it is the explanation both investigations prefer.
4. **Independent recovery is not central recovery.** In CrowdStrike, recovery paths independent of the crashed OS existed but required per-endpoint labour. Argument VI's independent seed activates in one step regardless of the number of units; the real paths do not. The model has no per-unit recovery cost, so it cannot represent the binding constraint the record names.
5. **Calm-period erosion (Theorem V.3) is not the mechanism NASA documents.** V.3's floor relaxes as a function of calm length, without shocks. NASA's thresholds relaxed after anomalies that were *survived*, which is a different mechanism (normalization after survived near-misses). Relaxation as a function of calm length is not identifiable in either program, and the comparable-shock premise fails for Challenger (36°F, 15°F below any prior launch; Rogers p.70).
6. **Calm-period erosion is untestable on public financial data.** No primary pre-crisis margin series exists in the sources checked. Where margins are measured (tri-party, from July 2008), they were stable and the failure was a run.
7. **Structural concession (Theorem V.2) is the only mechanism with documented components in its predicted form.** Boeing documents dated near-term-benefit choices and a persistent capability dependency. But the most consequential features trace in the record to the hazard classification, not to cost savings, and the "scar" was repaired by a later capability move. In the four-class reading that is severity ≤ 2, not the severity-3 branch.
